## Hard competitive criticism

The market is not novel just because it uses Kuru; Kuru already has a trading interface, and other tools expose open orders/cancellation. The build must win reviewer attention with evidence that its order state remains correct after reload and external changes, and that cancellation outcomes are independently verified. If that flow is missing, reduce the claim and do not present the project as meaningfully differentiated.

No claims are made about entrant count, market share, active users or competitor quality.
